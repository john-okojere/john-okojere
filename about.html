About John Okojere

Technology leadership, grounded in engineering.

John Nnaemeka Okojere is Chief Technology Officer and Technical Lead at Digi02 Tech Systems. He holds a Bachelor of Engineering in Information Communication Engineering from the Air Force Institute of Technology, Kaduna.
